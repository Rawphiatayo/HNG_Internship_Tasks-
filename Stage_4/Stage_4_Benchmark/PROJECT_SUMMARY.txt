================================================================================

This comprehensive feasibility study demonstrates that achieving ₦1 billion
in annual profit from palm oil production in Nigeria is FEASIBLE but requires:

✓ Significant scale (1,339-5,602 hectares depending on scenario)

✓ Operational excellence (21%+ OER, 18-21 MT/ha yields)

a prudent path to validate assumptions before full-scale commitment.
