# PALM OIL FEASIBILITY STUDY - DELIVERABLES
## Nigerian-German Investor - Palm Oil Profitability Analysis

### 📁 PROJECT OVERVIEW
This comprehensive feasibility study analyzes palm oil plantation profitability in Nigeria,
specifically addressing: "At what scale and in which states will a palm oil plantation 
generate ₦1 billion in annual profit?"

### 📊 KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY

**Bottom Line Up Front:**
- **Base Scenario:** 5,602 hectares required, ₦18.0B investment, 5.6% ROI, 21-year payback
- **Optimistic Scenario:** 1,339 hectares required, ₦4.4B investment, 22.8% ROI, 7.4-year payback
- **Top States:** Cross River (#1), Ondo (#2), Edo (#3)
- **Recommendation:** Phased approach starting with 500-ha pilot in Cross River

### 🎯 METHODOLOGY

**Three-Scenario Analysis:**
1. **Conservative:** Lower yields (16 MT FFB/ha), 19% OER, ₦480K CPO price
2. **Base:** Moderate yields (18.5 MT FFB/ha), 21% OER, ₦520K CPO price  
3. **Optimistic:** High yields (21 MT FFB/ha), 23% OER, ₦560K CPO price

**State Adjustments:**
- Rainfall patterns (optimal: 2000-3000mm)
- Infrastructure quality (0-10 scale)
- Land costs by location
- Labor availability

### 💼 KEY RECOMMENDATIONS

**Recommended Strategy:**
- **Phase 1 (Years 1-3):** 500-ha pilot in Cross River, ₦1.5-2.0B investment
- **Phase 2 (Years 4-7):** Scale to 1,339 ha, additional ₦2.8-3.0B investment
- **Target:** ₦1B annual profit by Year 7

**Critical Success Factors:**
- Achieve 21%+ oil extraction rate
- Maintain 18-21 MT/ha FFB yields
- Secure competitive pricing through forward contracts
- Build strong community partnerships

### ⚠️ KEY ASSUMPTIONS & LIMITATIONS

**Assumptions:**
- Palm oil prices stable at ₦480,000-560,000/MT
- Standard 3-year palm maturation period
- Consistent rainfall patterns
- Adequate labor availability
- No major pest/disease outbreaks

**Limitations:**
- State yields estimated using agro-climatic proxies
- Market volatility not fully modeled
- Political/regulatory risks not quantified
- Specific site soil analysis not conducted
